# Breeze

Breeze is a wallet for sending money to the people you know. Keep a balance, add money from your card,
send it to someone by their email, and take it back out to your bank — with every payment saved in a
clear history you can always look back on.

**Try it live:** [https://breeze-wallet.netlify.app](https://breeze-wallet.netlify.app)

## What you can do

- **Make your wallet yours** — sign up with an email, add your name, city and a profile picture.
- **Add money from your card** — pick an amount and pay through a secure checkout page.
- **Send money to anyone** — all you need is their email. If they don't have a wallet yet, the money
  is waiting the moment they join.
- **Take money out to your bank** — link your bank once through a guided setup, then withdraw any time.
- **Money in your language** — rupees, dollars, euros or pounds, and everything on screen follows.
- **Your own speed limits** — cap how much can leave your wallet per payment or per day.
- **A history that adds up** — see money in and money out, filter by type, and download everything as
  a spreadsheet file.
- **A watchful eye** — if a payment looks unusual for you, you'll see a heads-up straight away, and
  anything truly strange is stopped before it moves.
- **Extra sign-in safety** — turn on a one-time code from a free phone app, see every device you're
  signed in on, and sign out everywhere with one tap.
- **Forgot your password?** — a reset link lands in your inbox in seconds.

## Try it out

Two ready-made accounts to click around with, password `demo1234`:

- `demo@wallet.local` — the main account
- `friend@wallet.local` — a friend to send money to

Everything here is play money. The card step uses Stripe's test mode, so nothing is ever really
charged and nothing real moves.
